# 27. The Third Stair

The tide receded far enough to walk to Cinder Point without a boat. Ilse went back to the lighthouse and knelt beneath the third stair. The oilcloth was still there, and the brass astrolabe inside it had not rusted. She carried it out into the light while Tamsin watched. Now the harbor would have to believe the survey, and Ilse would have to explain how she had known where to look.
